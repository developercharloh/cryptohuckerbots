---
name: VIXUS AI VIP visibility
description: Product boundary between server/admin VIP qualification controls and user-facing signal/referral copy.
---

VIP qualification and activation rules remain enforced server-side and available in the admin product, but the user app must not expose VIP levels, qualification targets, activation amounts, or qualification-based referral rewards.

**Why:** The product decision is to keep qualification controls operational without turning the user experience into a VIP qualification or activation screen.

**How to apply:** Use neutral copy such as “Signal access unavailable,” “Signal access paused,” and “Invite friends and earn extra bonuses.” Keep referral/share functionality when useful, but omit declared bonus amounts, VIP columns, tier names, and qualification requirements from user routes.
